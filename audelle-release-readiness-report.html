Release hardening

Anonymous workload bounds

Per-client rate limits, request-body limits including chunked bodies, concurrency controls, output caps, and bounded lock/cache registries constrain expensive media operations.

Media boundary

Only HTTPS Googlevideo media hosts are accepted, redirects are disabled, MP3 preparation is bounded, and byte-range responses support Safari playback.

Container and network

The pod runs non-root with restricted capabilities, resource bounds, deny-by-default egress, and ingress limited to Audelle's tunnel identity.

Delivery integrity

CI runs backend, frontend, Chromium/WebKit E2E, and advisory checks. Published images include SBOM and provenance and production pins the reviewed digest.

Future release gate

  1. Merge only after CI and review are green.
  2. Record the source commit and immutable image digest.
  3. Promote through reviewed GitOps and wait for Argo CD to report Synced/Healthy.
  4. Verify public TLS, readiness, playlist generation, WebKit playback, next-track state, MP3 ranges, ZIP download, and cancellation.
  5. Observe tunnel and workload health; restore the prior digest through GitOps if checks regress.

Never expose Uvicorn, a NodePort, or Sohim's address publicly. Do not place personal YouTube cookies in production secrets.
